    How the numbers work. Completed, PO received, Invoiced and Paid cases count toward quota; Scheduled cases show as booked ahead; Cancelled cases count nowhere. Run rate = month-to-date actual ÷ business days elapsed × business days in the month (Monday to Friday, holidays not removed, today counted as elapsed). New-business-only, legacy-only and product-specific quotas only count matching cases. Draw plan (Brandon, Felipe): $3,000 monthly draw against a $20,000 monthly quota; a month that finishes under quota carries the shortfall into the next month's target for the rest of the quarter, and a month over quota banks nothing. Commission plans: Brandon 15% and Felipe 10% on new-business sales above a $20,000 monthly floor (the floor rises by any carried shortfall), with a $3,000 monthly draw; Abraham 10% on all sales above a $35,000 monthly floor, with a $5,417 monthly base salary; Preston 12% on Skeletal Dynamics sales from the first dollar, with a $6,000 monthly base salary and a SIM360 quota of 2 SIM Lab events per quarter. Commission figures are month-to-date estimates. Company quota is the team quotas plus rep quotas in territories with no team quota.
