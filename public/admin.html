Reps and plans

Quota and commission rules drive the scorecard, pace alerts and commission estimates. Counts limits quota to new or legacy surgeons; Product limits it to one product line. Carry adds a month's shortfall to the next month's target within the quarter. A Team row sets a quota for a whole territory. Turning access off signs the rep out right away.

    Target visits · all reps

    Every planned target-surgeon visit. When a rep checks in, their phone's location at that moment is compared with the facility address and the visit time. Green = at the facility during the visit. Red = somewhere else, outside the time window, or no check-in; you also get a push notification (turn on notifications in the tracker's Weekly digest card). Amber = the facility has no address yet, so it can't be checked.

    + Schedule a visit for a rep
    If the surgeon is not yet one of that rep's targets, they are added as a target.

    Facilities & addresses

    The address each check-in is compared against. Type the address and it is found on the map automatically, or stand at the facility and use Use my location. The radius is how far from that point still counts as "at the facility" (large hospital campuses need more).

    Price book

    Contract price per unit. When a rep logs a case, net sales fill in as units × contract price, and any case priced differently is flagged off contract. Leave facility blank for the default price; add a row with a facility name for that facility's contract. Product must match the case-log name.

    Product (case-log name)Facility (blank = all)Unit price $
    Paste many prices at once

    One per line: product, facility, price (facility can be empty: Clarix Flo,,1250). Existing rows for the same product and facility are replaced.

    Alert settings

    When the tracker flags billing, pathway and credential problems.

    Close a month

    Closing locks that month's cases so commissions can't change after you pay them. Reps see the month as read-only. Reopen to fix something; every change is kept in each case's history.

    Weekly digest

    Each Monday morning everyone who turned on notifications gets their week: pace to quota, POs to chase, next steps due, credentials expiring. You get the team version. Reps turn it on in the tracker (Weekly digest card).
